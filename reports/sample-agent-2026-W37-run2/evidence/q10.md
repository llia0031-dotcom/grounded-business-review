SQL：
```sql
SELECT o.pay_week, r.refund_type, o.category, COUNT(*) AS n, ROUND(SUM(r.refund_amount),1) AS refund_amt FROM fact_refund r JOIN order_wide o ON o.order_id=r.order_id WHERE r.refund_date >= '2026-08-24' AND r.refund_date <= '2026-09-13' AND o.category IN ('美发美甲') GROUP BY 1,2,3 ORDER BY 1,2
```
